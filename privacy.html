Privacy Policy

The short version: your notes are yours. Filament doesn't run servers that see them, and doesn't collect or sell your data.

Last updated September 5, 2026

Where your notes live

By default, everything you write in Filament is stored only on your device. If you turn on Filament Sync, your notes are also stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit — the same mechanism Apple's own apps use to sync data between your devices. Filament does not operate any servers of its own, and has no access to your notes at any point, synced or not.

Suggested Connections and AI drafting

Filament Sync also offers Suggested Connections between notes and AI-assisted drafting. Both features run entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence. Your note content is never sent to Filament or to any third-party AI service to generate these suggestions.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind in Filament.
  • We don't collect, view, or have the technical ability to access your notes.
  • We don't share or sell any data, because we don't collect any to begin with.

Subscriptions and payment

Filament Sync is billed and managed entirely through your Apple Account using StoreKit. Filament never sees or handles your payment details — that information is held by Apple, subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export any of your notes as Markdown or PDF at any time from Settings. Deleting a note removes it from your device (and from iCloud, if Filament Sync is on) — there is no separate copy anywhere else for us to delete, because we never had one.

Children's privacy

Filament is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since Filament's whole approach is to avoid collecting data in the first place, we don't expect that to change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to support@filamentnotes.app.
